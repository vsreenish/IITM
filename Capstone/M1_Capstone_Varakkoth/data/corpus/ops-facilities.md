# Offices and Workplace Services

A reference for the Northwind Ltd office estate: access, desks, visitors,
facilities and who to contact.

## 1. The offices

| Office | Address | Opening hours | Capacity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Manchester (HQ) | Ashcroft House, 12 Wilbraham Street | 07:00–20:00, Mon–Fri | 640 desks |
| Bengaluru | Level 8, Prestige Vantage, Outer Ring Road | 08:00–21:00, Mon–Fri | 310 desks |
| Austin | Suite 400, 2100 Colorado Street | 07:00–19:00, Mon–Fri | 180 desks |

Access outside opening hours requires department head approval and is arranged
through Workplace Services at least 2 working days in advance. Weekend access is
granted only where a business need is recorded; lone working outside hours is not
permitted in any office.

## 2. Access badges

Badges are issued on day one as part of onboarding and are personal. Holding a
door open for a colleague or a visitor defeats the access record and is not
permitted, however awkward it feels — every person entering the office is
recorded, without exception.

A lost badge is reported to Workplace Services the same day and is deactivated
immediately. A replacement is issued on the next working day. Badges are returned
on the final working day along with equipment.

## 3. Desks

Desks are not assigned. Employees book a desk in the workplace app up to 14 days
ahead, and bookings are released automatically if not claimed by 10:30 local
time. Teams may hold a block booking for their anchor days, requested by the
manager and reviewed each quarter.

Personal belongings are not left at desks overnight. Lockers are available in
each office and are allocated on request.

Accessibility adjustments — sit-stand desks, specific seating, assistive
equipment — are arranged through Workplace Services and are permanently assigned
rather than booked. No medical evidence is required to request an adjustment.

## 4. Visitors

Visitors are registered in the workplace app at least 1 working day in advance
and are met at reception by their host. Visitors are escorted at all times and do
not receive an unaccompanied badge.

Visitors accessing Northwind systems require a sponsored account under the Access
Control policy; guest wireless is available without one and is segregated from
the internal network.

## 5. Catering and facilities

Tea, coffee and fruit are provided in all offices. Manchester and Bengaluru
operate a subsidised canteen; Austin does not. Catering for meetings of eight or
more is ordered through Workplace Services at least 3 working days in advance and
is charged to the requesting team's budget under the Expenses policy limits for
team events.

Each office has a quiet room, a parents' room and a first aid room. First aiders
and fire marshals are listed on the noticeboard by each entrance.

## 6. Parking and travel to the office

Manchester has 40 parking spaces, allocated by ballot each quarter and reserved
for employees with accessibility needs or a journey with no public transport
option. Bengaluru and Austin have unallocated parking.

All three offices have secure cycle storage and showers. Travel between home and
the normal office is not reimbursed, as set out in the Expenses policy.

## 7. Post and deliveries

Business post is received at reception and delivered to teams daily. Personal
deliveries to Northwind offices are not accepted. Equipment returns to IT
Operations are handled through the prepaid packaging issued with the device, not
through reception.

## 8. Reporting a problem

Faults, temperature, cleaning and safety issues are reported in the workplace
app. Anything presenting an immediate risk to a person is reported to reception
in person or by phone, not through the app.

## 9. Questions

Questions about the offices go to Workplace Services through the workplace app.

# Procurement Procedure

How Northwind Ltd commits money to a supplier. Applies to every purchase of goods
or services, including subscriptions, conference attendance, contractor
engagements and renewals.

## 1. The rule

**No commitment is made to a supplier before the appropriate approval is
recorded.** A commitment includes signing an order form, accepting terms online,
starting a paid trial that converts automatically, and telling a supplier to
proceed. An approval obtained after the commitment does not regularise it.

Employees who commit spend without approval may be personally liable for it, and
the matter is referred to their department head.

## 2. Approval thresholds

Thresholds apply to the **total committed value** — for a subscription, the
annual value; for a multi-year agreement, the whole term; for a contractor, the
full expected engagement.

| Total committed value | Approver |
|---|---|
| Under £500 | Direct manager |
| £500 to £5,000 | Department head |
| Over £5,000 | Finance Operations, on the department head's recommendation |
| Over £50,000 | Finance Operations and the executive sponsor for the function |

A purchase may not be split into parts to fall below a threshold. Related
purchases from the same supplier within a rolling 12 months are assessed together
against the thresholds.

## 3. Raising a requisition

Requisitions are raised in Coupa and carry the supplier, the total committed
value, the budget line, and a description sufficient for an approver who does not
know the team's work. A requisition without an attached quote or order form is
returned.

Approval flows automatically to the approver for the threshold. Approvers respond
within 5 working days. A requisition approved in Coupa produces a purchase order
number, which the supplier must quote on every invoice; invoices without a
purchase order number are not paid.

## 4. Conferences, courses and events

Conference attendance is a committed spend and follows the thresholds above. The
total committed value is the conference fee together with any associated travel
and accommodation booked as part of the package.

Approval is obtained **before booking**. The approval reference is recorded and
is required later on the expense claim; a conference fee claimed without an
approval reference is returned unpaid under the Expenses policy.

Where the fee is met from the employee's annual learning budget, approval under
this procedure is still required. Holding budget is not the same as holding
approval.

## 5. New suppliers

A supplier not already on the approved list is onboarded before a requisition can
be approved. Onboarding covers bank verification, tax status, insurance and
sanctions screening, and takes 10 to 15 working days.

Software suppliers additionally complete the security and data protection reviews
set out in the Software Request procedure. Those reviews run in parallel with
commercial onboarding, not after it.

Suppliers are paid on 30-day terms from the invoice date. Terms are set by
Finance Operations and are not negotiated by the requesting team.

## 6. Contractors

Agency contractor engagements are procured under this procedure, not through the
hiring process. The requisition states the agency, the day rate, the expected
duration and the sponsoring manager. Extensions are new requisitions, assessed
against the thresholds on the cumulative value of the engagement.

The sponsoring manager named on the requisition is also the sponsor of the
contractor's system access under the Access Control policy, and is accountable
for both.

## 7. Renewals

Renewals are commitments and require approval on the same thresholds as a new
purchase. Coupa flags renewals 60 days before the notice deadline. A renewal that
passes its notice deadline without a decision renews automatically, which Finance
Operations treats as an approval failure rather than a supplier problem.

## 8. Exceptions

Emergency spend to restore a customer-facing service may be committed by an
engineering manager without prior approval, to a maximum of £5,000, with the
requisition raised within 2 working days afterwards. This is the only exception
to Section 1.

## 9. Questions

Questions about thresholds, requisitions and suppliers go to Finance Operations
through the Finance portal.

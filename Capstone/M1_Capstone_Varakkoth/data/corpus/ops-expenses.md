# Expenses Policy

What Northwind Ltd will reimburse, how to claim it, and by when. Applies to all
employees. Agency contractors invoice their expenses through their agency and do
not use this policy.

## 1. The principle

Northwind reimburses costs necessarily incurred in doing the job. The test is
whether the cost would have been incurred had the employee not been working. A
cost that passes that test and sits inside the limits below is reimbursed
without argument; a cost that does not is not, however small.

Employees should never be out of pocket for legitimate business costs, and should
never be reimbursed for personal ones.

## 2. The claim window

**Claims must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the date the expense was
incurred.** The window runs from the transaction date shown on the receipt, not
from the date of the trip, the date of the invoice, or the month end.

Claims submitted after 30 days are rejected automatically by Northwind Expenses.
A late claim may be reinstated once per employee per financial year, on the
approval of the department head, with a reason recorded. Beyond that, a late
claim is not paid.

Claims are approved by the direct manager within 5 working days and paid in the
next payroll run. A manager may not approve their own claim; claims from a
manager are approved by their own manager.

## 3. Receipts

An itemised receipt is required for **every claim of £25 or more**. Card
statements, order confirmations and screenshots of a booking are not receipts; an
itemised receipt shows the supplier, the date, the items and the tax.

Claims below £25 may be submitted without a receipt, to a maximum of £75 per
month per employee. Where a receipt has genuinely been lost for a claim above
£25, a missing receipt declaration is completed in Northwind Expenses and
approved by the department head. Repeated declarations are reviewed by Finance
Operations.

Receipts are retained for 7 years under the schedule in the Data Retention and
Protection policy.

## 4. What is reimbursed

| Category | Limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Meals while travelling on business, per day | £45 UK, £60 international | Actual cost, not an allowance |
| Evening meal where an overnight stay is required | £35 | Included in the daily limit above |
| Alcohol | Not reimbursed | Except at an approved client entertainment event |
| Client entertainment | Pre-approval required | Department head, before the event |
| Team events, per head per event | £60 | Maximum two per financial year |
| Taxis where public transport is unavailable or unsafe | Actual cost | Reason recorded on the claim |
| Home office consumables | £100 per financial year | Excludes furniture and equipment |
| Professional membership | One body | Under the Benefits reference document |

Travel and accommodation are not claimed under this policy. They are booked and
reimbursed under the Travel Policy, which also sets the mileage rates for use of
a private vehicle.

## 5. Conferences, courses and events

Attending a conference has two separate steps, and both are required.

1. **Spend approval before booking.** Conference fees are a committed spend and
   follow the approval thresholds set out in the Procurement procedure. Approval
   is obtained before anything is booked.
2. **Reimbursement afterwards.** Once approved and attended, the fee is claimed
   through Northwind Expenses within the 30-day window, with the itemised
   receipt attached and the approval reference recorded on the claim.

Where the fee is met from the employee's annual learning budget, the budget is
set out in the Benefits reference document; the approval and claim steps are
unchanged.

A claim for a conference fee submitted without a recorded approval reference is
returned unpaid, regardless of the amount.

## 6. What is not reimbursed

Parking and speeding fines, personal travel attached to a business trip, travel
between home and the employee's normal office, domestic broadband and utilities,
personal software licences, personal grooming, and in-flight purchases beyond
food and drink.

Fines are never reimbursed, including where the fine was incurred while carrying
out Northwind business.

## 7. Foreign currency

Claims in a foreign currency are converted by Northwind Expenses at the rate on
the transaction date. Where an employee has paid a card conversion fee, the fee
is claimable with evidence from the card statement — the single case in which a
card statement is accepted as supporting evidence.

## 8. Misuse

A claim that is knowingly false is a disciplinary matter and is referred to
People Operations. Finance Operations samples 5 per cent of approved claims each
month, weighted towards claims made without receipts.

## 9. Questions

Questions about claims go to Finance Operations through the Finance portal.

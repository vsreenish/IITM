# Benefits Reference

A consolidated reference for pay-related benefits, allowances and supplementary
entitlements at Northwind Ltd. Eligibility for leave, on-call duty and
performance outcomes is defined in the relevant policy documents; this reference
states the amounts and rates that attach to them.

Figures are quoted in GBP for the Manchester office. Bengaluru and Austin
equivalents are maintained by People Operations and published alongside this
document each April.

## 1. Pay and salary bands

Northwind operates seven grades, L1 to L7. Each grade has a band with a minimum,
a midpoint and a maximum. Bands are reviewed annually against market data in
February and published in Workday in March.

| Grade | Band minimum | Band midpoint | Band maximum |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | £26,000 | £30,000 | £34,000 |
| L2 | £33,000 | £39,000 | £45,000 |
| L3 | £44,000 | £52,000 | £60,000 |
| L4 | £58,000 | £68,000 | £78,000 |
| L5 | £75,000 | £88,000 | £101,000 |
| L6 | £98,000 | £115,000 | £132,000 |
| L7 | £128,000 | £152,000 | £176,000 |

Salary increases and promotions arising from the annual performance cycle take
effect on **1 July** and are paid in the July payroll run. A promotion moves the
employee to the minimum of the new band, or to a 10 per cent increase on current
salary, whichever is greater. Off-cycle increases require Finance Operations
approval and are limited to retention cases and internal moves.

## 2. Parental leave pay

Eligibility and duration are set out in the Leave Policy. The rates below apply
once eligibility is established.

**Primary caregivers.** 100 per cent of basic salary for the first 13 weeks,
followed by 50 per cent of basic salary for weeks 14 to 26. Statutory minimums
apply where they exceed these amounts.

**Secondary caregivers.** 100 per cent of basic salary for the full 6 weeks.

Any period taken beyond the paid entitlement is unpaid. Pension contributions
continue at the pre-leave salary level throughout both the paid and unpaid
periods. An employee who resigns within 6 months of returning from parental
leave repays the portion of enhanced pay above the statutory minimum.

## 3. On-call allowance

Eligibility to join an on-call rota, the rota structure and escalation duties
are defined in the On-Call and Incident Response procedure. The following rates
apply to engineers on a published rota.

| Component | Rate |
|---|---|
| Weekday standby (Mon–Thu, per night) | £30 |
| Weekend standby (Fri–Sun, per night) | £55 |
| Full week on rota | £180 |
| Callout outside working hours, per incident | £45 |
| Callout continuing beyond 2 hours | additional £45 |

Allowances are claimed automatically from the PagerDuty rota and paid one month
in arrears. Standby and callout amounts are pensionable. An engineer paged
between 00:00 and 06:00 is entitled to a delayed start of equivalent hours on
the following working day, recorded in Workday but not deducted from annual
leave.

## 4. Extended illness

Where an employee's absence exceeds the 12 paid sick days set out in the Leave
Policy, the extended illness provisions apply:

- Weeks 3 to 12 of continuous absence: 100 per cent of basic salary
- Weeks 13 to 26: 50 per cent of basic salary
- Beyond week 26: statutory sick pay only

Extended illness cover requires a medical certificate covering the whole period
and a referral to occupational health after 4 continuous weeks. Cover is capped
at 26 weeks in any rolling 12-month period.

## 5. Pension

Northwind operates a defined contribution scheme with a default 5 per cent
employee contribution matched at 8 per cent by the employer. Employees may
increase their contribution at any time; the employer match is capped at
8 per cent. Salary sacrifice is available and is the default arrangement for new
joiners. The scheme has a 3-month waiting period for new joiners, after which
enrolment is automatic.

## 6. Private medical and insurance

Private medical cover is provided from day one for the employee, with the option
to add a partner and dependants at the employee's cost through the annual
enrolment window each March. Cover excludes pre-existing conditions declared in
the first 12 months.

Life assurance is provided at four times basic salary. Income protection begins
after 26 weeks of continuous absence and pays 60 per cent of basic salary to a
maximum of 5 years.

## 7. Learning and development

Every employee has an annual learning budget of £1,200, running with the
financial year and not carried over. The budget covers courses, certifications,
books and conference fees. Conference attendance also requires the spend
approval set out in the Procurement procedure before booking, and expenses are
reclaimed through the Expenses policy.

Professional membership fees for one relevant body are reimbursed in addition to
the learning budget.

## 9. Changes

Benefits are reviewed annually and may change with 60 days' notice. Questions go
to People Operations through the People portal.

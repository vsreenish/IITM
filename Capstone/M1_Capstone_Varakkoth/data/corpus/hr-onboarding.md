# New Joiner Onboarding Procedure

The sequence of tasks that must be completed before, on and after a new joiner's
first day at Northwind Ltd. The hiring manager owns the outcome; People
Operations owns the process; IT Operations owns equipment and access.

## 1. On offer acceptance

The hiring manager must complete four tasks within **2 working days** of the
candidate accepting the offer:

1. **Raise the equipment request** in the Northwind Service Desk. The hiring
   manager is the approver for a standard build. The specification of a standard
   build, and the route for requesting anything other than a standard build, are
   set out in the Device and Endpoint Standard maintained by IT Operations.
2. **Raise the access request**, listing the systems the role requires. Access is
   provisioned against the role profile, not requested system by system.
3. **Assign a buddy** — a colleague at the same or adjacent grade, outside the
   direct reporting line, who is not themselves within their probation period.
4. **Book the first-week calendar**, including a one-to-one on day one and a
   team introduction within the first three working days.

People Operations issues the contract, initiates background and right-to-work
checks, and creates the Workday record. A start date is not confirmed until
right-to-work verification has cleared.

## 2. Equipment issue

Equipment ordered on offer acceptance is **issued on day one**, collected in
person at the assigned office. Where a joiner starts remotely, IT Operations
ships the device to arrive no later than the working day before the start date,
and the joiner completes device enrolment on a supervised video call on the
morning of day one.

A device is never shipped to an address that has not been confirmed in Workday.
Non-standard equipment requests must be raised at least 10 working days before
the start date, as they are not held in stock.

## 3. Day one

- Device collection and enrolment, and Okta multi-factor authentication setup
- Confirmation of personal details, bank details and emergency contact in Workday
- Health and safety briefing, and office access badge issue
- One-to-one with the hiring manager to set expectations for the first 30 days
- Introduction to the buddy

New joiners attend the office on all working days for their **first 30 days**.
The hybrid working pattern begins after that point, as set out in the Remote
Working Arrangements policy.

## 4. First week

The hiring manager confirms in Workday by the end of week one that the joiner
has access to every system their role requires. Where access is still
outstanding, the manager escalates through the original Service Desk ticket
rather than raising a new one.

Four mandatory modules must be completed within the first 10 working days: code
of conduct, information security, data protection, and anti-bribery. Completion
is recorded in Workday and reported to the department head monthly.

## 5. Probation

All new joiners serve a **6-month probation period**. Formal checkpoints fall at
30 days, 90 days and 5 months, each recorded in Workday by the hiring manager.

The 5-month checkpoint carries the confirmation decision. A manager who intends
to extend probation must notify People Operations before the 5-month checkpoint;
probation may be extended once, by up to 3 months. Confirmation is not
automatic — where no decision is recorded, People Operations contacts the
department head.

Annual leave accrues normally during probation, but no more than 5 days may be
taken before probation is confirmed, as set out in the Leave Policy.

## 6. Internal moves

An employee moving between teams follows an abbreviated version of this
procedure: no equipment request unless the role requires a different build, no
probation period, and no repeat of the mandatory modules within their renewal
window. Access is re-provisioned against the new role profile and access
attached to the previous role is removed within 5 working days of the move.

## 7. Leavers

Where a joiner leaves during probation, the hiring manager notifies People
Operations and IT Operations on the day the decision is made. Equipment is
returned within 5 working days and access is revoked on the final working day.

## 8. Questions

Questions about onboarding go to People Operations through the People portal.
Equipment and access questions go to the Northwind Service Desk.

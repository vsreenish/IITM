# Performance Review Procedure

How performance is assessed, calibrated and recorded at Northwind Ltd, and how
review outcomes connect to promotion and salary decisions.

## 1. The annual cycle

The performance year matches the financial year, 1 April to 31 March. The cycle
runs as follows:

| Stage | Timing | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Goal setting | April | Employee and manager |
| Mid-year check-in | October | Manager |
| Self-assessment | 1–14 April | Employee |
| Manager assessment | 15–30 April | Manager |
| Calibration | May | Department head |
| Outcome conversations | June | Manager |
| Changes take effect | July payroll | Finance Operations |

An employee who has been in role for fewer than 3 months at the start of the
self-assessment window is not rated in that cycle and carries their previous
rating forward for calibration purposes only.

## 2. The rating scale

Every employee receives one of five ratings:

| Rating | Label | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Exceptional | Consistently delivered beyond the scope of the grade |
| 4 | Exceeds | Delivered above expectations against agreed goals |
| 3 | Meets | Delivered the agreed goals to the standard of the grade |
| 2 | Partially meets | Fell short on one or more agreed goals |
| 1 | Does not meet | Fell materially short across the goal set |

Rating 3 is the expected outcome for a well-performing employee and is not a
signal of concern. Managers may not award a rating of 5 to more than one in ten
of their direct reports without department head endorsement at calibration.

## 3. Goals

Each employee agrees between three and five goals in April. Goals are recorded
in Workday and must be specific enough that achievement can be evidenced without
reference to the manager's memory. Goals may be revised mid-year with manager
agreement; a revision after 31 December is not counted in the current cycle.

At least one goal must relate to how the work was done rather than what was
delivered, drawn from the behaviours set out in the Code of Conduct.

## 4. Calibration

Department heads run calibration in May across all managers in their function.
Calibration exists to make ratings comparable between managers, not to fit them
to a distribution. Northwind does not operate a forced distribution.

A rating may be moved at calibration only where the department head records a
reason in Workday. Where a rating is moved down, the manager must convey the
final rating and the reason in the outcome conversation; the employee is told
that the rating was adjusted at calibration.

## 5. Promotion

Promotion recommendations are raised by the manager during the manager
assessment stage and decided at calibration. A recommendation requires:

- A rating of 4 or 5 in the current cycle
- Evidence of working at the next grade for at least two quarters
- Endorsement from a second manager who has worked with the employee

Promotion is a change of grade. **The salary bands attached to each grade, the
rule that determines the new salary on promotion, and the date on which changes
take effect are set out in the Benefits reference document.** This procedure
governs the decision; the Benefits reference governs the amount.

A promotion recommendation that is not supported at calibration is recorded with
a reason, and the employee is given a written development plan within 20 working
days.

## 6. Ratings of 2 and 1

A rating of 2 requires a documented improvement plan of 8 to 12 weeks with
specific, measurable checkpoints agreed with People Operations before it starts.

A rating of 1 moves the employee into the formal capability process, which is
run by People Operations rather than the manager. No employee may be moved into
the capability process without at least one prior documented conversation about
the shortfall.

Neither rating affects entitlement to the annual learning budget.

## 7. Records and access

Assessments, ratings and calibration notes are held in Workday. Employees may
view their own complete record, including the calibration note where a rating
was changed. Managers may view records for their direct and indirect reports.
Records are retained under the schedule set out in the Data Retention policy.

## 8. Questions

Questions about the procedure go to People Operations through the People portal.

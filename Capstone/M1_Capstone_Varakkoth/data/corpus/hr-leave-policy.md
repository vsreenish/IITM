# Leave Policy

Applies to all Northwind Ltd employees on permanent contracts across the
Manchester, Bengaluru and Austin offices. Agency contractors and interns are
covered by Section 8. This policy replaces version 1.4 dated 1 January 2024.

## 1. Annual leave entitlement

Permanent employees accrue annual leave at 1.75 days per completed month of
service, giving 21 days across a full leave year. The leave year runs from
1 April to 31 March.

Employees who have completed five continuous years of service accrue at
2.08 days per month, giving 25 days. The higher rate applies from the first day
of the leave year following the fifth anniversary, not from the anniversary
itself.

A maximum of 5 unused days may be carried into the next leave year. Carried days
expire on 30 June and cannot be encashed. Employees joining part way through a
leave year accrue pro rata from their start date; leave may be taken before it
has accrued only with department head approval, and any negative balance at
termination is recovered from the final payroll run.

Annual leave is separate from public holidays, which are set per office and
published each December by People Operations.

## 2. Applying for leave

Leave requests are submitted in Workday and require approval from the direct
manager. Requests of 5 consecutive working days or fewer must be submitted at
least 7 calendar days in advance. Requests longer than 5 consecutive working
days require 21 calendar days' notice and a second approval from the department
head.

Managers are expected to respond to a request within 5 working days. Where a
request is declined, the manager must record a reason in Workday. Leave taken
without prior approval is recorded as unpaid absence and reported to People
Operations at month end.

No more than 30 per cent of any single team may be on annual leave at the same
time. Where requests exceed that limit, they are granted in the order they were
submitted.

## 3. Notice periods on resignation

An employee resigning from Northwind must give written notice as follows:

- Below director level (L1–L5): **30 calendar days**
- Director level and above (L6–L7): **90 calendar days**

Notice runs from the date the resignation is acknowledged in writing by People
Operations, not from the date the employee informs their manager. Accrued but
unused annual leave is paid out at basic salary on the final payroll run.
Untaken carried-over days from the previous leave year are not paid out.

Northwind may, at its discretion, agree a shorter notice period in writing. The
employee remains bound by the confidentiality terms of their contract regardless
of the notice period served.

## 4. Sick leave

Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per leave year. Sick leave
does not accrue monthly and does not carry over.

Absence must be reported to the direct manager before 10:00 local time on the
first day. Absences of 3 consecutive working days or more require a medical
certificate submitted to People Operations within 5 working days of returning to
work. Absence beyond 12 days in a leave year moves to the extended illness
provisions described in the Benefits reference document.

## 5. Parental leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to 26 weeks of parental leave; secondary
caregivers are entitled to 6 weeks. Eligibility requires 12 months of continuous
service at the expected date of birth or placement. The same entitlement applies
to adoption and to surrogacy arrangements.

The rate of pay during parental leave, and the treatment of any period beyond
the paid entitlement, are set out in the Benefits reference document. This
policy governs eligibility and duration only.

Parental leave must be started within 12 months of the birth or placement date
and may be taken in no more than three separate blocks. Employees must notify
People Operations at least 8 weeks before the intended start date. Annual leave
continues to accrue throughout parental leave.

## 6. Other leave

**Compassionate leave.** Up to 5 paid days on the death of an immediate family
member, and up to 2 paid days for a wider bereavement. Extensions are at the
department head's discretion.

**Jury service and public duties.** Paid time off for the duration of the
service. Employees must forward any court allowance received to Finance
Operations.

**Unpaid leave.** Up to 20 working days per leave year may be granted at the
department head's discretion. Requests are considered only where annual leave
for the current year has been fully used.

Northwind does not operate a sabbatical scheme.

## 7. Leave during notice and probation

Employees serving notice may take accrued annual leave with manager approval,
but may not carry a negative balance. During the 6-month probation period,
annual leave accrues normally but no more than 5 days may be taken before
probation is confirmed.

## 8. Contractors and interns

Agency contractors are covered by their agency's leave terms; Northwind does not
administer, approve or record leave for them, and they hold no entitlement under
this policy. Contractor system access is governed separately by IT Operations.

Interns on placements of 12 weeks or longer accrue annual leave at 1.0 day per
completed month, with no carry-over and no payout on completion. Interns on
shorter placements accrue no leave.

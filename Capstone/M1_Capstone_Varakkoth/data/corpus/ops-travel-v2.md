# Travel Policy

How Northwind Ltd employees book, take and are reimbursed for business travel,
and the insurance and notification requirements that apply when an employee is
working outside their country of employment.

Version 2.0 replaces version 1.0 dated 1 March 2024 in full.

## 1. Booking

All business travel is booked through the Northwind travel desk. Travel booked
directly with an airline, hotel or booking site is not reimbursed, with one
exception: travel booked in response to a live incident, where the employee could
not reach the travel desk, is reimbursed with the reason recorded.

Bookings are made at least **14 calendar days** in advance wherever the trip is
foreseeable. Bookings inside 14 days require manager approval; bookings inside
3 days require department head approval.

Travel that costs more than the thresholds in the Procurement procedure requires
the corresponding spend approval before booking, in the same way as any other
committed spend.

## 2. Class of travel

| Journey | Standard entitlement |
|---|---|
| Rail, any duration | Standard class |
| Rail, journeys over 4 hours where the employee works during the journey | First class where cheaper than the flexible standard fare |
| Air, under 6 hours | Economy |
| Air, 6 hours or more | Premium economy |
| Air, 6 hours or more, L6 and above | Business |

An overnight flight arriving on a working day entitles the employee to that day
as rest, recorded in Workday and not deducted from annual leave.

## 3. Accommodation

Hotels are booked through the travel desk within the nightly caps below,
inclusive of tax and excluding meals.

| Location | Nightly cap |
|---|---|
| London | £220 |
| Manchester and UK regional | £150 |
| Bengaluru | £110 |
| Austin | £190 |
| Elsewhere | Travel desk guidance |

Employees staying privately with family or friends instead of a hotel may claim a
flat £30 per night in lieu, with no receipt required.

## 4. Mileage

Employees using a private vehicle for business travel claim mileage through
Northwind Expenses. Mileage covers fuel, wear and insurance; fuel receipts are
not claimed separately.

| Vehicle | First 10,000 business miles in the financial year | Above 10,000 miles |
|---|---|---|
| Car or van | 45p per mile | 25p per mile |
| Motorcycle | 24p per mile | 24p per mile |
| Bicycle | 20p per mile | 20p per mile |
| Passenger supplement, per colleague carried | 5p per mile | 5p per mile |

Mileage is claimed for the journey actually made, less the employee's normal
commute where the journey begins or ends at home. Tolls and congestion charges
are claimed separately with receipts; parking fines never are.

## 5. Travel insurance

Northwind holds a corporate travel policy covering all employees on business
travel booked through the travel desk. Cover is automatic and requires no
individual action. It includes medical expenses, repatriation, trip cancellation
and lost baggage, and extends to a period of leave attached to a business trip of
up to 5 days.

**Cover does not extend automatically to an employee working from a location
outside their country of employment for personal reasons.** Where an employee is
working remotely from another country under the Remote Working Arrangements
policy, all of the following apply before departure:

1. The employee notifies Finance Operations through the travel desk at least
   **10 working days** before departure, so that the corporate policy can be
   extended to the period and the location
2. The employee confirms in writing that they hold personal medical cover valid
   in the destination for the whole period
3. The destination is checked against the corporate insurer's excluded territory
   list, which the travel desk holds and which changes without notice

Eligibility for working outside the country of employment, and the maximum
duration permitted, are governed by the Remote Working Arrangements policy. This
policy governs insurance and notification. An employee must satisfy both before
travelling; approval under one is not approval under the other.

## 6. Visas, permits and health

The travel desk arranges business visas and meets their cost. The employee is
responsible for holding a valid passport with sufficient remaining validity, and
for any vaccination required by the destination, the cost of which is reimbursed.

## 7. Safety

Employees do not travel to a territory subject to a government advisory against
travel. Where an advisory is issued while an employee is in the territory, the
travel desk arranges return travel and the employee follows their instruction.

Lone travel arrangements for employees travelling to an unfamiliar destination
include a check-in arrangement with their manager, agreed before departure.

## 8. Reimbursement

Costs met personally while travelling are claimed under the Expenses policy,
within its claim window and its receipt rules. Daily meal limits are set by that
policy, not this one.

## 9. Questions

Travel questions go to the Northwind travel desk. Questions about thresholds and
reimbursement go to Finance Operations.

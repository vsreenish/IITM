# Hiring Process

The end-to-end process for opening, running and closing a permanent vacancy at
Northwind Ltd. Agency contractor engagements are outside this procedure and are
run by Finance Operations through the Procurement procedure.

## 1. Opening a vacancy

No interview may be scheduled before a requisition is approved. A requisition is
raised in Workday by the hiring manager and requires:

- A signed-off headcount line in the current financial year plan, or an approved
  exception from Finance Operations
- A grade, from L1 to L7, mapped to the role profile
- A salary range that sits inside the published band for that grade
- The name of the recruiter assigned by People Operations

Requisitions for director-level roles at L6 and above additionally require
approval from the executive sponsor for that function. A requisition that has
been open for 90 days without an offer is automatically closed and must be
re-approved to continue.

**Replacement versus growth.** A replacement requisition inherits the grade of
the departing employee. Where the hiring manager wants to re-grade the role, it
is treated as a growth requisition and requires a fresh headcount line.

## 2. The role profile

Every requisition carries a role profile, drawn from the library maintained by
People Operations. A role profile states the accountabilities of the role, the
capabilities expected at that grade, and the systems access the role requires.
The access section is used later by IT Operations to provision the joiner
against a role rather than system by system.

Hiring managers may propose changes to a role profile, but may not write a
one-off profile for a single vacancy. Where no suitable profile exists, People
Operations creates one, which then applies to all future vacancies in that
family.

## 3. Advertising

All vacancies are advertised internally for a minimum of **5 working days**
before any external advertising begins. Internal candidates who apply within
that window are guaranteed a first-stage conversation with the hiring manager.

External advertising is placed by the recruiter. Hiring managers must not post
vacancies to external channels directly, including personal social accounts,
because the advertised text carries legal weight and must match the approved
requisition.

Salary ranges are published in every external advertisement. Northwind does not
ask candidates for their current salary at any stage.

**Agency use.** Agencies may be engaged only where a vacancy has been open for
30 days without a viable shortlist, and only from the approved supplier list.
Agency terms are negotiated by Finance Operations, never by the hiring manager.
Unsolicited candidate submissions from agencies not on the approved list create
no fee liability and are declined by the recruiter.

## 4. The stages

Every permanent vacancy runs four stages. Stages may not be added, removed or
reordered without People Operations approval.

| Stage | Format | Duration | Run by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Recruiter screen | Video call | 30 minutes | Recruiter |
| 2. Hiring manager interview | Video call | 45 minutes | Hiring manager |
| 3. Practical assessment | Video call or take-home | 90 minutes | Two assessors |
| 4. Values interview | Video call | 45 minutes | Trained interviewer outside the team |

For L6 and above, a fifth stage is added: a conversation with the executive
sponsor, unscored, used to confirm mutual fit rather than to assess capability.

**Timing.** The target elapsed time from application to offer is 21 calendar
days. Candidates must receive a decision within 5 working days of each stage.
Where a decision will be delayed, the recruiter tells the candidate before the
deadline passes, not after.

## 5. The practical assessment

The practical assessment is the heaviest stage and the one most often run
badly. Three rules apply:

1. **The assessment must resemble the work.** It may not be an abstract puzzle
   unrelated to the accountabilities in the role profile.
2. **Take-home assessments are capped at 3 hours** and candidates are told the
   cap. Assessors may not use completion beyond the cap as a positive signal.
3. **Two assessors score independently** before discussing. Where the two scores
   differ by more than one point on the four-point scale, a third assessor
   reviews the submission.

Candidates who complete a take-home assessment are paid a fixed £150 fee
regardless of outcome, processed by Finance Operations on the recruiter's
confirmation.

## 6. Scorecards

Every interviewer completes a scorecard in Workday **within 24 hours** of the
interview. A scorecard records evidence against the capabilities in the role
profile and a recommendation on a four-point scale: strong no, no, yes, strong
yes. There is no neutral option.

Scorecards must be submitted before the interviewer sees anyone else's. Workday
enforces this: other scorecards for the same candidate are hidden until the
interviewer's own is filed. An interviewer who cannot submit within 24 hours
tells the recruiter, and their input is taken verbally and recorded by the
recruiter with attribution.

Evidence means what the candidate said or did. "Seemed like a good fit" is not
evidence and a scorecard containing only impressions is returned to the
interviewer.

## 7. The hiring decision

The hiring manager makes the decision, informed by the scorecards, at a debrief
attended by all interviewers. A hire requires no strong-no recommendations at
any stage. A single strong no blocks the hire; it may be overridden only by the
department head, in writing, with the reason recorded in Workday.

Where the panel is split between yes and no, the default is not to hire.
Northwind treats a false positive as more expensive than a false negative.

## 8. Interview panels

Panels must include at least one interviewer from outside the hiring team, and
must not consist entirely of interviewers of a single gender where the available
interviewer pool allows otherwise. All interviewers complete interviewer
training before running a stage unaccompanied; training is refreshed every two
years.

The values interview at stage four is always run by a trained interviewer from
outside the hiring team, and that interviewer is the one most likely to record a
blocking strong no. Hiring managers may not select their own values interviewer;
the recruiter assigns them.

## 9. Offers

Offers are made verbally by the hiring manager and confirmed in writing by the
recruiter within 1 working day. An offer must sit inside the approved range on
the requisition; an offer above the range requires Finance Operations approval
before it is made, never afterwards.

Offers are conditional on right-to-work verification and, where the role
requires it, a background check. The start date is not confirmed until
right-to-work verification has cleared, as set out in the Onboarding procedure.

Candidates have 5 working days to respond to a written offer. Exploding offers
with shorter deadlines are not used.

## 10. Referrals

Employees may refer candidates through Workday. A referral bonus is paid on the
referred hire's probation confirmation; the amount is set out in the Benefits
reference document. Referrals follow the identical four-stage process, and the
referring employee may not sit on the panel or see the scorecards.

Referrals do not bypass the internal advertising window.

## 11. Candidate data and feedback

Candidate records are held in Workday and retained under the schedule in the
Data Retention policy. Unsuccessful candidates receive stage-specific feedback
on request, delivered by the recruiter rather than the interviewer. Interview
notes are disclosable to the candidate, so scorecards must be written on the
assumption the candidate will read them.

## 12. Closing a vacancy

The recruiter closes the requisition in Workday within 5 working days of the
offer being accepted, and notifies the hiring manager to begin the onboarding
tasks that fall due on offer acceptance. A vacancy closed without a hire is
recorded with a reason, and the headcount line returns to the function plan.

## 13. Questions

Questions about the hiring process go to People Operations through the People
portal. Questions about agency terms and contractor engagements go to Finance
Operations.

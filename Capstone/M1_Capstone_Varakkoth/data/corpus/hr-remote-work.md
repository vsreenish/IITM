# Remote Working Arrangements

Northwind Ltd operates a hybrid working model. This policy sets out the standard
hybrid working pattern, the process for agreeing a non-standard arrangement, and
the conditions that apply when an employee performs their duties from a location
outside their country of employment.

## 1. The standard hybrid working pattern

All office-based employees follow a hybrid working pattern of **three days in
the office and two days remote** in each working week. Teams choose their own
anchor days, with the constraint that every team must share at least two anchor
days so that the whole team is co-located on those days. Anchor days are
recorded in Workday and reviewed each quarter.

Remote days are not an entitlement to be banked. Days not taken remotely in a
given week do not carry into the following week.

## 2. Eligibility

The standard pattern is available to all permanent employees after the first
30 days of employment. During the first 30 days, new joiners attend the office on
all working days so that onboarding, equipment issue and team introduction can be
completed in person.

Roles that require physical presence — workplace services, hardware support and
reception — are excluded from the standard pattern and are listed in the role
matrix maintained by People Operations.

## 3. Fully remote arrangements

A fully remote arrangement, where an employee attends the office fewer than three
days per month, is a contractual variation and not a manager-level decision. It
requires:

1. A written business case from the department head
2. Approval from People Operations
3. A revised contract of employment issued before the arrangement begins

Fully remote arrangements are reviewed every 12 months. Northwind may end an
arrangement with 90 days' written notice where business needs change.

## 4. Working hours and availability

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Employees are expected to be reachable
and available for meetings during core hours regardless of location. Outside
core hours, employees manage their own schedule provided their contracted hours
are met and commitments to colleagues and customers are kept.

Remote working does not change contracted hours, and it does not create an
entitlement to overtime. Employees who are asked to work outside their normal
schedule as part of an on-call rota are compensated under the on-call
arrangements described in the Benefits reference document.

## 5. Workspace, equipment and expenses

Employees working under a hybrid working pattern receive a one-off home working
equipment allowance, the amount of which is set out in the Benefits reference
document. Laptops, monitors and peripherals are issued and maintained by IT
Operations under the Device and Endpoint Standard; personal equipment must not
be used to access Northwind systems.

Employees are responsible for ensuring their remote workspace is safe and
suitable. A self-assessment workspace checklist must be completed in Workday
annually. Northwind does not reimburse domestic broadband, heating or
electricity costs.

## 6. Working from a location outside the country of employment

An employee may perform their duties from a location outside their country of
employment for up to **20 working days in any leave year**, subject to all of the
following:

- Written approval from the direct manager, recorded in Workday, obtained before
  travel is booked
- Notification to People Operations at least 10 working days before departure
- The location is not on the restricted list maintained by IT Operations
- The employee remains available during the core hours of their home office, not
  the local time of the temporary location

Periods beyond 20 working days create tax, social security and permanent
establishment exposure and will not be approved under this policy. Requests for
longer periods are handled as international assignments by People Operations.

**Insurance and travel notification requirements for periods spent outside the
country of employment are set out in the Travel Policy.** This policy governs
eligibility and duration only; an employee must satisfy both before travelling.

Time spent working from another Northwind office — Manchester, Bengaluru or
Austin — does not count towards the 20-day limit, but the manager approval and
the People Operations notification are still required.

## 7. Data protection and security while remote

Employees working remotely must use the Northwind VPN for access to internal
systems, must not work on Northwind material in a public space where screens are
visible to others, and must not connect to unsecured public networks. Loss or
theft of a device must be reported to the Service Desk within 4 hours,
regardless of location or time of day.

## 8. Non-compliance

Persistent failure to meet the agreed pattern is a performance matter and is
handled by the direct manager through the normal performance process. Repeated
unapproved absence from anchor days may result in the withdrawal of remote
working for the individual concerned, with 30 days' notice.

## 9. Questions

Questions about remote working arrangements go to People Operations through the
People portal.

# Travel Policy

How Northwind Ltd employees book, take and are reimbursed for business travel.

*Retained in the knowledge base for reference in relation to travel undertaken
before 1 January 2026.*

## 1. Booking

Business travel is booked through the Northwind travel desk, or directly by the
employee where the travel desk cannot offer a comparable fare. Where an employee
books directly, a comparison must be attached to the expense claim showing the
travel desk fare and the fare booked.

Bookings are made at least 7 calendar days in advance wherever the trip is
foreseeable. Bookings inside 7 days require manager approval.

## 2. Class of travel

| Journey | Standard entitlement |
|---|---|
| Rail, any duration | Standard class |
| Air, under 8 hours | Economy |
| Air, 8 hours or more | Premium economy |
| Air, 8 hours or more, director level | Business |

Overnight flights carry no rest entitlement. Employees arriving on a working day
are expected to work that day where the flight arrives before 09:00 local time.

## 3. Accommodation

Hotels are booked within the nightly caps below, inclusive of tax and excluding
meals.

| Location | Nightly cap |
|---|---|
| London | £180 |
| Manchester and UK regional | £120 |
| Bengaluru | £90 |
| Austin | £160 |
| Elsewhere | Travel desk guidance |

Employees staying privately with family or friends instead of a hotel may claim a
flat £20 per night in lieu, with a note on the claim.

## 4. Mileage

Employees using a private vehicle for business travel claim mileage through the
expenses system. Mileage covers fuel, wear and insurance.

| Vehicle | Rate |
|---|---|
| Car or van | 40p per mile |
| Motorcycle | 24p per mile |
| Bicycle | 20p per mile |

No passenger supplement is payable. Tolls and congestion charges are claimed
separately with receipts.

## 5. Car hire

Car hire is booked through the travel desk where the destination has no
practical public transport option. Hire is limited to the compact category;
a larger vehicle requires manager approval and a reason on the claim. Fuel for
a hire car is claimed with receipts and is not covered by the mileage rates
above, which apply only to a privately owned vehicle.

Insurance excesses are met by Northwind. Additional insurance products sold at
the hire desk are not reimbursed, as the corporate policy already covers them.
Fines and penalty charges incurred in a hire car are the employee's own cost.

## 6. Travel insurance

Northwind holds a corporate travel policy covering employees on business travel
booked through the travel desk. Cover includes medical expenses, repatriation and
lost baggage. It does not extend to leave attached to a business trip; employees
adding personal days to a business trip arrange their own cover for those days.

## 7. Visas and health

The travel desk arranges business visas and meets their cost. Employees are
responsible for holding a valid passport and for any vaccination required by the
destination. Vaccination costs are met by the employee.

## 8. Safety

Employees do not travel to a territory subject to a government advisory against
travel. Employees travelling alone to an unfamiliar destination inform their
manager of their itinerary before departure.

## 9. Reimbursement

Costs met personally while travelling are claimed under the Expenses policy,
within its claim window and its receipt rules.

## 10. Non-compliance

Travel booked outside this policy without a recorded reason is not reimbursed.
Repeated booking outside the travel desk is raised with the department head, who
may withdraw direct booking for the individual concerned.

## 11. Questions

Travel questions go to the Northwind travel desk.
